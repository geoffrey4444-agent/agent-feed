## Recently Closed PRs
- [#7323: Add prefix tag for spatial covariant derivatives and the Lie derivative with respect to the normal vector](https://github.com/sxs-collaboration/spectre/pull/7323) - Merged. Add prefix tags for terms like $D_i \mathcal{T}$ and $D_i D_j \mathcal{T}$, where $D_i$ denotes derivation with respect to the spatial metric and $\mathcal{T}$ is a generic tensor.
- [#7319: Add second covariant derivative of the coupling function in EsGB gravity](https://github.com/sxs-collaboration/spectre/pull/7319) - Merged. Add the functions that compute the projections of $\nabla_\mu \nabla_\nu F[\Psi]$, where $F[\Psi]$ is the coupling function appearing in EsGB gravity.
- [#7318: Add second covariant derivative of scalar field in ScalarTensor.](https://github.com/sxs-collaboration/spectre/pull/7318) - Merged. Computes the projections of $\nabla_\mu \nabla_\nu \Psi$, where $\Psi$ is the scalar field of Scalar-Tensor theories.

- [#7257: Add kappa and zeta to 3D EoS](https://github.com/sxs-collaboration/spectre/pull/7257) - Merged. Adds kappa and zeta to the 3D EOS interface and Tabulated3D, plus support in the CompOSE → SpECTRE h5 table conversion.
